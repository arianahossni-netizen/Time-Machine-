Welcome to the Time Portal

guia soundous

Hello, explorer! Choose a year from 1000 to 3000. The time machine will show a short history and a related image for the year you visit, then take you on a pretend trip through time.

How to use it

  • Enter the year you want to visit.
  • Press “Start Trip” and wait for the story and image to appear.
  • Use “Show image” and “Show story” in the side panel to switch between the picture and its history.
  • Use the buttons to open doors, control lights and sound, or test the alarm.

Rules and tips

  • Choose a year from 1000 to 3000.
  • Past events are short learning notes. Some years may show general information.
  • Years after 2026 are guesses, not confirmed facts; their pictures are approximate illustrations, not real photos from the future.
  • This is a pretend experience for fun and learning. It is not a real time machine.
